Official Nodal Agency

Press Information Bureau

The Press Information Bureau (PIB) is the nodal agency of the Government of India to disseminate information on government policies, programmes, initiatives and achievements to the print, electronic, and digital media.

PIB functions as an essential bridge between the Union Government and the public, facilitating communication through press releases, press conferences, media briefings, official photo archives, and the specialized PIB Fact Check Unit.

PIB Fact Check Unit

Don't believe or forward unverified government notices! PIB Fact Check counters misinformation, identifies viral fake claims, and verifies authentic government circulars.

Viral Fake Alert · Debunked

Claim: Ministry of Finance charging ₹250 fee for filing ITR acknowledgment slips via WhatsApp.

Fact: This claim is completely FAKE. No such charge is levied by the Ministry or Income Tax Department.
