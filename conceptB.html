FABRICATED NOTICE Reviewed · 12 August 2026

Claim: Ministry of Railways is issuing free lifetime travel passes under Amrit Bharat Scheme

Official Fact Check: The circulated order is completely counterfeit. Indian Railways has introduced no such concession. Official notifications are issued exclusively via indianrailways.gov.in.

PHISHING FRAUD Reviewed · 10 August 2026

Claim: Ministry of Finance is disbursing ₹5,000 subsidy via WhatsApp APK download link

Official Fact Check: This is a credential-harvesting banking scam. The Government of India never distributes financial assistance or subsidies through direct APK file downloads.

COUNTERFEIT APPOINTMENT Reviewed · 06 August 2026

Claim: Appointment letter demanding ₹1,850 fee for Assistant Director post in Ministry of Power

Official Fact Check: Central Government recruitments are managed exclusively through constitutional recruiting bodies (UPSC and SSC). No ministry collects fees through personal UPI or QR codes.
