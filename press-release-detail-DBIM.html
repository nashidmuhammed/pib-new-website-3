Digital India BHASHINI Division (DIBD) and NITI Aayog Sign Statement of Intent to Advance Multilingual AI for Inclusive Governance

Key Highlights for Media & News Desks

  • National Multilingual Architecture: Statement of Intent (SoI) formalizes integration of voice-first Indian language AI across all 28 States and 8 Union Territories.
  • Aspirational Districts Priority: Vernacular voice assistance deployment prioritized across 112 Aspirational Districts to overcome literacy barriers in welfare service delivery.
  • Full 22 Scheduled Languages: BHASHINI APIs will provide automated speech recognition (ASR), neural machine translation (NMT), and text-to-speech (TTS) across 22 constitutionally recognized languages.

NEW DELHI | PIB: In a landmark milestone aimed at democratizing digital public services across linguistic barriers, the Digital India BHASHINI Division (DIBD) under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) and NITI Aayog signed a comprehensive Statement of Intent (SoI) today at the NITI Aayog headquarters in New Delhi.

The strategic collaboration aims to leverage frontier Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Natural Language Processing (NLP) models to deliver citizen-centric public governance in India's regional and native languages. Under this partnership, the BHASHINI linguistic engine will be integrated natively into central policy dashboards, aspirational district grievance monitoring tools, and social welfare distribution portals.

Accelerating Inclusive Governance in 22 Scheduled Languages

Addressing the gathering, the Secretary, Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology, highlighted that language should never be an impediment for any citizen seeking access to government welfare, agricultural advisories, health consultations, or digital financial services.

"India’s diversity is reflected in our vibrant linguistic heritage. With BHASHINI and NITI Aayog joining hands, we are ensuring that every citizen in the farthest aspirational district can speak to government services in their mother tongue and receive prompt, authentic, and empathetic support."

— Senior Representative, Ministry of Electronics & IT

The initiative will focus on three key transformation pillars:

  • Voice-First Vernacular Interfaces: Enabling citizens to use voice queries to apply for certificates, pensions, crop insurance, and scholarships.
  • Multilingual Knowledge Synthesis: Real-time translation of central research papers, scheme guidelines, and best practices into all 22 official languages.
  • State & District Level Capacity Building: Dedicated sandboxes for state governments to train localized conversational agents on district-level public data.
Implementation Roadmap for Multilingual AI Integration
Phase Target Timeline Scope of Vernacular Deployment Target Coverage
Phase I Q4 2026 Aspirational Districts & Blocks monitoring 112 Districts
Phase II Q2 2027 State Portal integrations across Agriculture, Health & Education All 28 States
Phase III Q4 2027 Panchayat-level voice kiosks and offline edge-AI translation nodes 2.5 Lakh Gram Panchayats

The meeting concluded with a pledge to establish a joint Project Management Unit (PMU) between NITI Aayog’s Frontier Tech Vertical and the Digital India BHASHINI Division to ensure rigorous monitoring of outcomes and transparent milestones.
